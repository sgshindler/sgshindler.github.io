excerpt: 'The post-synthesis surface reaction of vapor-deposited polymer thin films is a promising technique in engineering heterogeneous surface chemistry. Because the existing research has neglected marginally reactive precursor films in preference of their highly reactive counterparts, our knowledge of kinetics and loss of film integrity during the reaction are limited. To address these limitations, we characterize hydrolysis of two fluoroacrylates, poly(1H,1H,2H,2H-perfluorooctyl acrylate) (pPFOA) and poly(2,2,3,4,4,4-hexafluorobutyl acrylate) (pHFBA), with sodium hydroxide using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy.'
